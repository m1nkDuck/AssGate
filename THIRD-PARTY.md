# Third-party tools and asset provenance

These tools are provided separately under their original licenses. They are not embedded in the game JAR; the game contains AshGate classes, its manifest, and the hero and boss animation atlases.

- Eclipse ECJ 3.21.0 — Eclipse Foundation; EPL-2.0, see the original `about.html` extracted to `licenses/ECJ-about.html`. Source: https://github.com/eclipse-jdt/eclipse.jdt.core ; https://archive.eclipse.org/eclipse/downloads/
- ProGuard 6.2.2 — Guardsquare; GPL-2.0, license in `licenses/`. Corresponding source: https://github.com/Guardsquare/proguard/tree/v6.2.2
- MicroEmulator 2.0.4 (`microemulator.jar`, `cldc.jar`, `midp.jar`) — MicroEmulator contributors; LGPL, license in `licenses/`. Source: https://github.com/tisoft/microemu and https://sourceforge.net/projects/microemulator/files/microemulator/2.0.4/

The knight atlas `res/hero-atlas.png` contains six actions, with frame coordinates and anchors in `assets/knight-sheets/frames.json`. Roll and death use the previously supplied source sheets. Walk uses `assets/knight-sheets/knight_walk_classic.png`, an AI recoloring of the corrected walk cycle from the user's supplied image to match the original muted armor palette. Idle, attack, and heal use `knight_idle_matched.png`, `knight_attack_matched.png`, and `knight_heal_matched.png` in the same directory, AI-generated to match that walk character's armor, colors, and proportions. Attack retains the sword in the right hand; heal raises the potion in the left hand while retaining the sword in the right. The four new source images have 24 frames each; their source SHA-256 values are recorded in the metadata and verified during packing. The supplied source assets retain their original ownership.

Packing reads the four new sheets' individual crop rectangles and anchors, removes the exterior black matte by flood-fill while retaining enclosed black armor details, and applies nearest-neighbor scaling by direction. Ground anchors are aligned after resampling to prevent fractional scales from shifting planted soles. Attack has per-row column bounds to retain complete sword poses. The four sheets already use down/left/right/up row order and set `mirror_profiles: false`; legacy death profile mirroring remains enabled. The resulting 480x1536 atlas contains 144 cells of 80x64 pixels with a shared foot anchor at (40,52). Horizontal padding retains extended sword poses while preserving on-screen body positions. The atlas has binary transparency for MIDP; only the atlas is embedded in the game JAR.

The armored skeleton boss uses the AI-generated transparent sources `assets/boss-sheets/boss_skeleton.png` and `boss_skeleton_walk_fixed.png` in the same directory, styled to match the hero's muted plate while exposing ivory skull, ribs, spine, and limb bones. Its 32 poses comprise four directions with idle, four walking frames, sword windup, strike, and fallen poses. `tools/pack_boss_sprites.py` takes seven corrected walking poses with swords from the second source and retains the other 25 poses from the first. It extracts the isolated figures, applies nearest-neighbor scaling so the standing body is 88 pixels tall, and aligns all soles at (112,100) in 224x112 cells. The 1792x448 `res/boss-atlas.png` has binary MIDP alpha and retains extended sword poses. Left-facing windup and strike are mirrored from the right-facing source poses to correct their direction. Both source hashes and each frame's source filename, crop, and scale are recorded in `assets/boss-sheets/frames.json`. `src/BossSprites.java` adds hit recoil and a phase-two floor aura; `src/SkeletonBossArt.java` supplies original Java-drawn fallback art.

The `168-Knight.webp` image credited to SLYNYRD was supplied as an armor style reference; that reference image is not bundled in the game. Background art is drawn in Java source. Sound effects are generated tones; no recorded music is bundled.
